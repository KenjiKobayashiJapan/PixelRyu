Game Overview

A cozy number puzzle set in a tiny terrarium. Place number eggs into the nests, make each nest's total match its target exactly, and watch the eggs hatch into curious little Sumlings — collect them all as your terrarium fills with life.

The Terrarium

🌱

Your world is a small glass terrarium dotted with nests. Numbered eggs wait to be placed, each nest shows the total it's hoping for, and every puzzle you solve hatches a new Sumling — a gentle, wondrous creature of numbers — into your growing collection.

How to Play

🥚

Place, add, hatch. Read each nest's target, drop in the right number eggs, and make the sums line up — no timer, no rush, just a quiet little puzzle.

  1. Each nest shows a target total it wants to reach.
  2. Drag number eggs from your tray into the nests.
  3. Make every nest's total match its target exactly — no more, no less.
  4. Solve the board and the eggs hatch into Sumlings for your collection.
