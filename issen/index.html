  • Keep installed for the full 14 days
  • Launch every couple of days (a few times is enough)
  • A minute or two of play per session is plenty

  • Step 1 — Join the group: even if you see “no permission to view content”, there’s a “Join group” button on that page — tap it to join.
  • Step 2 — Opt-in, then install & play: after opting in, download the app from the Play Store, open it and play a little. Joining the group alone is not enough.

  • If Step 2 shows “App not available”, wait a few hours after joining and try again.
