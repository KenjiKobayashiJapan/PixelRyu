SENKO

Game Overview

SENKO is a cozy, two-phase smoke puzzle. Light a stick of incense and a restless dark smoke rises — sweep it with your finger to calm it until it settles and turns clear and white. Then guide that white smoke to fill a faint Japanese motif: the moon, a pine, a crane, cherry blossoms and more, across 120 quiet stages. Watch for red embers — touch one and it burns your smoke back, so weave around them. The smoke is finite, so shape it before the incense burns down. A fan calms broadly and a brush helps you shape. No pressure, 12 languages.

🕯 Out now on the App Store and Roblox; Android testers welcome on Google Play.

The Game

🕯

Light a stick of incense and a restless dark smoke rises. Sweep it with your finger to calm it until it settles and turns clear and white — that is phase one. Then keep sweeping to guide the white smoke into a faint Japanese motif and fill its shape — that is phase two. The smoke is finite: each stick of incense burns down, so finish the shape before it runs out.

Fan, Brush & Red Embers

🍃

Two tools help you shape the smoke: the fan (uchiwa) calms broadly, and the brush (fude) helps you place smoke exactly where you want it. Red embers drift through the smoke — touch one and it burns your smoke back, so weave around them. If the smoke gauge runs low, on mobile a rewarded ad refills it, while the Roblox edition uses coins you earn instead of ads.

How to Play

👆
  1. Sweep your finger through the rising smoke to calm the dark, churning smoke until it settles and turns white.
  2. Keep sweeping to gather the clear smoke into the faint motif and fill its shape — the fan calms broadly, the brush helps place smoke.
  3. Avoid the red embers and finish the shape before the incense burns down.
