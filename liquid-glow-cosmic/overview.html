Liquid Glow

Game Overview

A calm, luminous color-matching puzzle set in deep space. Slide glowing light along the constellation and bring each light to the star whose ring shows its color. Easy to learn, deeply relaxing, surprisingly deep.

The World

🌌

Neon light glows against a dark, starlit cosmos. Stars are joined by glowing threads, and custom in-engine lighting and particles make every move feel soft and satisfying — a quiet, ASMR-like place with no timers and no pressure.

How to Play

🧩

Tap a glowing star, then tap a connected empty star to slide its light along the threads. Bring each light to the star whose ring shows its color.

  1. Tap a glowing star to pick up its light.
  2. Tap a connected empty star to slide the light along the thread.
  3. Fill every ring with its matching color to clear the level — fewer empty stars means harder planning.
