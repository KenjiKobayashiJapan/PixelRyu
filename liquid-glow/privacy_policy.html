Privacy Policy — Liquid Glow

Effective date: 2026-05-11  |  Last updated: 2026-05-11

This Privacy Policy describes how PixelRyu ("we", "our", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Liquid Glow (the "App"). By installing or using the App you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We design Liquid Glow to collect only the minimum data necessary to operate and improve the App. We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email address, or phone number) directly through the App.

1.1 Device and usage data (collected automatically)

  • Device model, operating system and version
  • Anonymous device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • Approximate region (country-level only)
  • App crash reports and performance metrics
  • Aggregated gameplay statistics (level reached, session length, etc.)

1.2 Locally-stored data (not transmitted)

Game progress, audio preferences and similar settings are stored on your device only. We do not upload these to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — for serving in-app advertisements (banner / interstitial / rewarded / app open formats). See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — for displaying GDPR / consent forms in applicable regions.

3. Use of Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues and crashes
  • Serve relevant advertising (where permitted)
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Children's Privacy

Liquid Glow is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Your Rights (EU / UK Users — GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to or restrict certain processing (including personalized ads)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

The first time you launch the App in the EU/UK, a consent dialog (UMP) will let you choose how your data may be used for advertising.

6. Your Rights (California Users — CCPA / CPRA)

California residents may request information about the categories of personal information collected and request deletion. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Retention

Anonymous device-level analytics are retained only for as long as necessary to provide and improve the App, generally up to 26 months. Locally-stored progress remains on your device until you delete the App.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data transmitted to our analytics and advertising partners. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, however.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

当方は、本アプリの運営および改善に必要な最小限のデータのみを取得します。本アプリを通じて、氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号等の個人を直接特定できる情報を収集することはありません。

1.1 自動的に収集されるデバイス・利用データ

  • 集計済みのゲームプレイ統計（到達レベル、セッション長など）

1.2 端末内にのみ保存されるデータ

ゲーム進捗・音量設定等は、お使いの端末内にのみ保存され、当方サーバーへ送信されません。

本アプリは以下の第三者SDKを使用しており、それぞれのプライバシーポリシーに基づき情報を取得することがあります。

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — GDPR等の同意フォーム表示。

3. 情報の利用目的

  • 本アプリの運営・保守・品質向上

本アプリは一般ユーザー向けに提供されます。当方は13歳未満（または各地域で定められた最低年齢未満）の個人情報を意図的に収集することはありません。万一お子様が個人情報を提供したと思われる場合、下記連絡先までご連絡ください。
