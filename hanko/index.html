HANKO

Zen Seal Carving Logic Puzzle

Play now on Roblox; coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

HANKO is a quiet carving puzzle about a Japanese seal. Tap a cell to carve it away; whatever you leave uncarved stays raised, takes vermilion ink, and prints onto the washi paper below. Each stage fixes one to four guides on the paper: a cell covered by one guide prints light red, and where prints from two guides overlap the ink doubles into a deep dark red. Your print has to match the target exactly — blank, light, or dark. After each press you learn only how many cells came out too dark and how many too light, so you reason your way to the answer. Carving is one-way. 200 stages, a daily seal, and 12 languages.

FAQ

What kind of game is HANKO?

HANKO is a negative-space logic puzzle by PixelRyu. You carve grooves into a Japanese seal, press it onto washi paper, and the red ink you leave behind has to match a target pattern exactly.

Is HANKO free to play?

Yes — HANKO is free to play, supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases.

Where can I play HANKO?

Play HANKO on Roblox now; coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Follow PixelRyu for the release date.

How do you play HANKO?

Tap a cell on the seal face to carve it away. The cells you leave uncarved take ink and print inside every guide on the paper. A cell covered by one guide prints light red; where prints from two guides overlap, the ink doubles into a dark red. Press the seal and match the target's blank, light and dark cells exactly.

Which languages does HANKO support?

HANKO supports 12 languages: English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Indonesian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Russian.
