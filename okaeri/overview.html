Okaeri

Game Overview

Okaeri is a cozy pet homecoming slide puzzle. Slide a row or column of glassy food bowls; bowls wrap from one edge to the other. When a whole line becomes one pet's favorite food, that pet crosses the board. Then rotate the outer ring, where pets and owners wait, so the matching owner is on the far side. Reunite every pair to clear the stage. Calm stages, no timer, and 12 languages.

🏠 Okaeri is out now on Roblox — Android testers are welcome on Google Play; the App Store version is coming soon. Art shown here is from the game in development.

The Game

🥣

Swipe a row or column to slide glassy food bowls around the board — bowls wrap from one edge to the other. When a whole line becomes one pet's favorite food, that pet crosses the board. Reunite every pet with its matching owner to clear the stage; the stages are calm and there is no timer.

The Outer Ring

🏠

Pets and owners wait on the ring around the board. Rotate the outer ring so the matching owner is on the far side when a pet crosses — and reunite every pair, one homecoming at a time, across 60 quiet stages.

How to Play

🐾
  1. Swipe a row or column to slide the glassy food bowls; bowls wrap from one edge to the other.
  2. Make a whole line one pet's favorite food — that pet crosses the board.
  3. Rotate the outer ring so the matching owner waits on the far side, and reunite every pair to clear the stage.
