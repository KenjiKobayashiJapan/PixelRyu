📦 What it is

A cozy block puzzle where you lay tatami mats in a quiet Japanese room. Drag a mat onto the floor and complete a full row or column to finish that line. A samurai then carries the finished mats to the tatami magistrate, and delivering earns koban that buy extra lives. A light Haste gauge keeps you moving. With a lucky-laying bonus. 48 stages. Free, made by PixelRyu.
