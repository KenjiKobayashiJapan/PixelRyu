Game Overview

A cozy parcel-sorting puzzle from a tiny gift workshop. Sort cute parcels into three lanes, match each shape to its box on the belt, and ship a smile to a child somewhere — before your table overflows.

🎁 In development — art and screens shown here are not final.

The Workshop

📦

You're a small workshop hand. Toys and gifts roll in from the back room, your three sorting lanes hum quietly, and boxes loop by on a warm wooden belt. Every box that ships is a smile somewhere — that mint bear on the cover is the child waiting to open it.

How to Play

✋

Drag, match, ship. The belt brings the boxes; you steer the parcels. The table fills if you fall behind — so read what's coming and keep the flow.

  1. Drag each parcel from the table into one of three sorting lanes by shape.
  2. Watch the boxes loop past on the conveyor — each box wants a specific shape.
  3. When a matching box rolls by the front of a lane, the parcel pops in with a happy click.
  4. Fill a box, the helper ships it off, a fresh empty box rolls in. Chain ships in a row for combos.
  5. Keep the table from overflowing — use Slow, Cancel, Return, or Continue when it gets wild.
