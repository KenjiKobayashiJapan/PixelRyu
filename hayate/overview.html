Game Overview

A 3D katana speed-racer, and a direct sequel to ISSEN. Ride a hover-craft at full tilt across four worlds, weave through everything that flies at you, and throw your homing katana to slice the rest clean — the way of the single cut, now at speed.

The Four Worlds

🏁

One blade, four worlds — each with its own hazards and rhythm: a neon highway, the silence of outer space, the crushing ocean deep, and the open sky. Across dozens of stages and an endless mode, the road never stops rushing toward you.

How to Play

🗡️

Steer, dodge, and cut — all while the world races past. You choose your line and when to throw; the speed does the rest.

  1. Lean left or right to pick your line and weave between the lanes.
  2. Read what flies at you and slip cleanly through the gaps.
  3. Throw your homing katana with a tap — it seeks the nearest hazard, slices it clean, and returns to you.
  4. Chain clean dodges and cuts to keep your speed up and finish first.
