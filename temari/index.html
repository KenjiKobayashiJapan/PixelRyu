🏮 What it is

A first-person paddle breakout set in a cozy Japanese night. Move your hagoita (battledore) with a fingertip, keep the temari thread-ball alive in an endless rally, and pop every paper balloon down the corridor — with split balls, focus slow-motion, a pheasant helper and more. 8 worlds, 200 stages.
