WAGASHI

Cozy Japanese-Sweets Merge Puzzle
COMING SOON COMING SOON

Now on Roblox; coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

WAGASHI is a cozy Japanese-sweets merge puzzle. Drop a sweet into the vessel; when two of the same touch, they merge into the next, climbing a 10-step chain from a tiny azuki bean all the way to a grand kagami-mochi. Round sweets roll and settle with soft physics — just don’t let one come to rest above the warning line. Play it two ways: an endless, calm Zen mode, or 60 order stages where you fill a request within a limited number of moves. A fan and a pair of chopsticks help you nudge and re-place, and a “Next” preview lets you plan each drop. No pressure, 12 languages.

FAQ

What kind of game is WAGASHI?

WAGASHI is a cozy Japanese-sweets merge puzzle by PixelRyu. Drop a sweet into the vessel; when two of the same touch, they merge into the next one, climbing a 10-step chain from a tiny azuki bean to a grand kagami-mochi.

Is WAGASHI free to play?

Yes — WAGASHI is free to play, supported by optional ads. On Roblox there are no ads; you use coins you earn.

Where can I play WAGASHI?

WAGASHI is out now on Roblox; the App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon. Follow PixelRyu for the launch.

How do you play WAGASHI?

Move the drop position left or right and tap to drop a sweet. Two of the same merge into the next tier. Don’t let a sweet settle above the warning line at the vessel’s mouth. Use the fan to nudge the pile, the chopsticks to re-place a sweet, and watch the “Next” preview to plan ahead.

Which languages does WAGASHI support?

WAGASHI supports 12 languages: English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Indonesian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Russian.
