Game Overview

WAGASHI is a cozy Japanese-sweets puzzle, and nothing in it combines on its own. Dropping a sweet only sets out an ingredient: it rolls into the vessel and settles. When two of a kind come to rest side by side, a gold ring pulses around the cluster — press and hold and the whole cluster is kneaded at once into half as many sweets of the next kind, rounded down, so six become three. Just don't let a sweet come to rest above the warning line.

🎁 Out now on the App Store and Roblox. Android testers welcome on Google Play. — screens shown here are from the in-development build.

The Game

🍡

Move the drop position left or right and tap to set a sweet into the vessel. Sweets never combine on contact — dropping one only puts out an ingredient. When two of a kind come to rest side by side, a gold ring pulses around the cluster: press and hold and the whole cluster is kneaded at once into half as many sweets of the next kind. Kneading costs no drops, and running out of drops does not end the round — just don't let a sweet settle above the warning line at the vessel's mouth. Play it two ways: an endless, calm Zen mode, or 200 order stages where you fill a request within a limited number of drops. A “Next” preview lets you plan each drop.

The Fan & the Chopsticks

🥢

Each tool takes two steps: tap its icon to select it (the icon highlights, and you can lift your finger), then drag on the board to use it. With the uchiwa fan selected, drag across the board to send a gust through the pile that pushes the nearby sweets around (a bit unpredictable, like real fanning); with the chopsticks selected, drag to pick up one sweet you choose and set it down wherever you like. Because your score comes from how many sweets you fold into one knead and how tightly they were packed, the tools raise your score indirectly, by letting you gather the pile before you knead it. Your tools carry over from stage to stage and across both modes, chains of kneads earn you more, and an optional ad can refill them.

How to Play

🍵
  1. Move the drop position left or right and tap to drop a sweet.
  2. Press and hold a cluster of two or more of a kind — the whole cluster is kneaded into half as many of the next kind. Don't let a sweet settle above the warning line.
  3. Use the fan and chopsticks to gather the pile before you knead it, and watch the “Next” preview to plan ahead.
