CounterParts

Game Overview

CounterParts is a cozy, minimalist turn-based puzzle. One swipe moves two mirror twins at once — Gold, a warm golden glass drop, and Platinum, a cool silver-white glass drop — each sliding one cell in opposite, point-symmetric directions. A wall or the board's edge stops one twin while the other keeps going, and that desync is the whole puzzle: line both twins up so a single move brings them to rest together in their own matching-color holes. No death, no timer — just quiet, click-by-click thinking inside a little brass tally counter. 120 levels across 7 families.

🎁 Now on the App Store and Roblox — Android is in open testing on Google Play, testers welcome.

The Game

🧮

CounterParts is a turn-based puzzle played inside a little brass tally counter. Each swipe moves both mirror twins at once — Gold, a warm golden glass drop, and Platinum, a cool silver-white glass drop — one cell each in opposite, point-symmetric directions. Your goal: bring both twins to rest together in their own matching-color holes. Every move clicks the counter, and you can undo.

Walls & the Desync

🧱

A wall or the board's edge stops one twin while the other keeps going — and that desync is the whole puzzle. Use walls to break the twins' symmetry, line them up, and set up the single move that brings both to rest together. No death, no timer — just quiet, click-by-click thinking, with undo whenever you need it.

How to Play

👆
  1. Swipe — Gold and Platinum each slide one cell in opposite, point-symmetric directions.
  2. Use walls and the board's edge to stop one twin while the other keeps moving, and line the two up.
  3. Bring both twins to rest together in their matching-color holes to clear the level — every move clicks the tally counter, and you can undo.
