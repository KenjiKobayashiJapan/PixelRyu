Game Overview

モグラぽかぽか

Tap the moles within 30 seconds, reach each stage's target, and advance to the next — a stage-based tap action. 100 stages in all, getting faster as you go. A cozy little time-killer you can chip away at in spare moments.

The World

🌱

The stage is a grassy green field where comical moles pop up from holes here and there. No violence at all — a warm, lighthearted vibe that's friendly for all ages.

How to Play

🔨

Controls are simple. Each stage has a "target" number; hit at least that many moles within the 30-second limit to clear the stage.

  1. When the stage starts, a 30-second countdown and the target number appear.
  2. Quickly tap the moles popping out of their holes (the screen shows "score / target").
  3. Reach the target within the time limit for a "STAGE CLEAR!"
  4. Leftover time is added to your score as a Time Bonus (remaining seconds × 10).

Take on the Stages

🚩

Clear stage after stage and aim to conquer all 100.

  • 100 stages in all. Clearing one unlocks the next, and progress saves automatically.
  • As you progress, moles appear faster and stay up for less time, and the target rises — difficulty ramps up.
  • Didn't reach the target? Retry as many times as you like.
  • Jump back to any cleared stage anytime from "Stage Select."
